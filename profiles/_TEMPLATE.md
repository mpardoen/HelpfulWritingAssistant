# Profile: <name>

One line saying which kinds of work this profile is for.

To add a profile, copy this file to `profiles/<name>.md`, fill in each section, and put `profile: <name>` in the `BOOK.md` of the works that use it. The agents read the sections by their headings, so keep the seven `##` headings exactly as they are.

## Chapter record

The sections every `record.md` must have for this kind of work. Later consistency checks only see these records, never the earlier chapters themselves, so ask for whatever a later chapter could contradict: names, numbers, definitions, commitments.

## Consistency

What the proofreader compares between the new chapter and the earlier records.

## Style

What the proofreader looks at within the chapter, and anything it should leave alone.

## Reader review

The sections of a reader's comment on one chapter, ending with whatever verdict or rating suits this kind of work.

## Reader memory

The sections of what a reader keeps from a chapter. This is the only thing the reader will know of this chapter when reading the next one.

## Whole-work analysis

The rubric the editor follows after re-reading the entire work.

## Typical readers

Examples of reader personas worth creating for this kind of work.
